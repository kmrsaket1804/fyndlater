We've all been there. You're mid-conversation and want to share *that* reel — the one about sustainable packaging, or the recipe with the garlic trick, or the workout routine your friend needs. You open Instagram, tap Saved, and… scroll. And scroll. And give up.

## The Problem with Instagram's Saved Tab

Instagram's save feature was designed for quick bookmarking, not retrieval. There's no search, no smart sorting, and collections require manual effort nobody maintains.

The result? A graveyard of content you'll never find again.

## Why Our Brains Fail at This

Research on memory retrieval shows we remember *context* far better than exact content. You recall "that reel about the guy making pasta in the copper pot" — not the creator's handle or the date you saved it.

Instagram gives you reverse-chronological order. Your brain wants semantic search.
