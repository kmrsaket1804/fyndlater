When you send a reel to Faye, a lot happens in the background. Within seconds, that 30-second video becomes a rich, searchable entry in your personal knowledge base. Here's how.

## Step 1: Receive and Acknowledge

The moment Faye receives your DM, she sends back a confirmation. Behind the scenes, the reel URL is extracted and queued for processing.

## Step 2: Content Extraction

Faye's pipeline pulls multiple layers of information from the reel:

- **Audio transcription** — everything spoken in the video, including voiceovers and dialogue
- **Visual analysis** — key frames are analyzed for text overlays, products, locations, and context
- **Metadata** — creator info, caption text, hashtags, and engagement signals

## Step 3: Semantic Understanding

Raw transcripts and visual data get synthesized into a structured understanding:

- What is this content *about*?
- What categories does it fit?
- What are the key takeaways?
- What would someone search for to find this?

This is where AI shines — understanding intent and context, not just keywords.

## Step 4: Indexing and Storage

The processed content is embedded into a vector space (think: a map of meaning) alongside all your other saves. This enables natural-language search that understands synonyms, related concepts, and context.

## Step 5: Ready for Retrieval

Ask Faye "that marketing reel about hook formats" and she searches across meaning — not just matching words. She finds the right save even if you describe it differently than the original content.

## Why This Matters

Traditional bookmarking stores a URL. Faye stores *understanding*. That's the difference between a list you'll never revisit and a memory you can actually use.

Try it yourself — send a reel to Faye and then ask for it in your own words.
